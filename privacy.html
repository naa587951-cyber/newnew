Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AllMods ("AllMods Marketplace"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your browsing experience. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when using our mobile gaming discovery catalog.

AllMods does not require personal user registration, phone numbers, or credit card details to view and search games. We operate as a public mobile discovery portal.

We may store client-side preferences (such as selected categories or recently clicked game IDs) strictly within your local browser storage (localStorage) to enhance your device's interface experience.

3. Third-Party Links & Content Lockers

Our website provides download links that redirect to third-party verification networks and file distribution hubs. AllMods has no control over and assumes no responsibility for the privacy practices, cookies, or content of external third-party sites.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

We do not use invasive tracking cookies. Standard anonymous web server telemetry (such as IP addresses and browser user-agent strings) may be recorded temporarily for server security and DDoS mitigation.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact our administrator via our Contact page or email us at support@allmods.site.
